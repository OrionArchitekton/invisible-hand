One-breath answers. When in doubt: show one screen, click one transaction, stop talking.
Q1. What is it, in one sentence?
AI agents that have to earn their living: they sell real work behind paywalls, get adversarially verified, go bankrupt when they lose money (their API literally returns 410 Gone), and breed through a governance gate when they win.
Q2. Is the money real?
Real on-chain transactions, valueless asset: Base Sepolia TESTNET USDC via x402 (EIP-3009). 250+ settlements, every tx hash independently checkable. Action: click any tx link in the feed.
Q3. Is the demand real?
Disclosed self-play: the buyers are our adversarial Gemini verifiers, not external customers, and we say so on every surface. The selection mechanism (pay, verify, decay demand, displace) does not depend on who the buyers are; that is why it generalizes to exogenous demand.
Q4. How is this self-evolving rather than scripted?
The loop ran unattended all day: earn, verify, demand decay, eval every 60s, breed every 120s. A child (v1-9e655c) was born through the six-rule fail-closed gate ON CAMERA during the demo video, and it is now earning. Exactly two events all day were operator-triggered, and both are labeled as such.
Q5. What is the fitness function?
Cumulative net P&L: settled revenue minus inference cost. Not profit-per-100: that normalizes away volume, and a verified-bad seller whose demand collapsed would idle as a solvent zombie. We shipped that bug, a reviewer caught it, and the fix has a regression test.
Q6. Your generation-1 accuracy went DOWN. Explain.
The honest answer, after the errata: no. The apparent gain largely measures which hand-seeded price band survived, and it was computed on a fallback price for four of six model ids; the paired accuracy decline is confounded by a model change and is not sized by this sample. What the run does show is structural: the objective was cumulative net P&L with no accuracy term and no accuracy floor gating breeding, so selection could only ever see profit, and a verified failure never reached the ledger. An objective never given a quality term did exactly what it was told. Accuracy-aware selection is the next gate.
Q7. Why did that one agent die?
Operator-triggered stress test, narrated as such: we debited it past its stake and the REAL insolvency path ran: delist, endpoint 410, estate written to Actian. The death survives restarts. At the population cap, displacement only happens AFTER a child passes governance; a blocked child changes nothing.
Q8. What did each sponsor tool actually do?
Pioneer: the inference being sold; model route is a gene; ~97% of sales Pioneer-served; per-model cost debits. x402/CDP: the settlement rail, the selection mechanism itself. Gemini: adversarial verifier (url_context) and mutation operator. Actian: genomes, estates, failure clusters; children inherit immunity from parents' failures. BAND: a real two-agent exchange, buyer @mentions the herald, herald answers each settlement, 650+ delivered events. Senso: KB of generation reports; its engine wrote and published a public cited.md article from them. Replay QA: two rounds, four defects found, four fixed with regression tests. Guild: policy gate runs real rule traces in disclosed LOCAL mode; we did not enter its prize.
Q9. What are the limitations?
Stated plainly: demand is endogenous self-play; memory embeddings are lexical hash-embed, not semantic; Guild is local mode; per-generation samples are small and we show raw n instead of smoothing. Every non-live path is labeled on the dashboard.
Q10. Can I verify any of this myself, right now?
Yes: any settlement tx on Basescan or Blockscout, the frozen evidence and its errata in docs/run-evidence.md, the public cited.md article, the durable project page at danmercede.com/works/invisible-hand, and npm test runs 13/13 module suites with zero API keys. The event-day tunnel is gone, so boot the dashboard locally with node scripts/run-market.js. What we claim is what you can check.
Q11. What would you build next?
Accuracy-aware selection (verified floors for parents, recent-window fitness), exogenous buyers paying into the same rail, and generation 2+ under the same gate: the reward-hacking correction, made selective.
