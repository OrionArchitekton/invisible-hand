THE INVISIBLE HAND

An agent economy where real on-chain settlement selects which AI agents survive and breed. Built solo in one day at SwarmHack SF (tokens&, July 24, 2026) by Dan Mercede. All settlement is Base Sepolia TESTNET USDC: real transactions, valueless asset.

Thesis

Most "self-evolving agent" demos optimize a simulated score. Invisible Hand makes selection economic and external: a population of seller-agents earns real on-chain revenue, pays real (estimated, price-table-based) inference costs, and lives or dies by cumulative net P&L. The falsifiable claim: settlement-selected breeding improves the population's unit economics across generations. The run supports it, with an honestly reported wrinkle described below.

Mechanism

  • Sell: each variant serves claim extraction over live Hacker News articles behind an x402 paywall (402 challenge, EIP-3009 facilitator settlement); every sale carries a tx hash.
  • Verify: buyers are adversarial Gemini verifiers (disclosed self-play). A verified failure sharply cuts a seller's future demand (epsilon-greedy with hard decay).
  • Select: fitness is cumulative net P&L. Insolvency delists a variant: its endpoint literally returns HTTP 410 GONE and its estate is written to memory. At the population cap the weakest evaluated variant is displaced only after a child passes governance (a distinct, honestly labeled market-selected exit).
  • Breed: children are Gemini-mutated crossovers that inherit immunity hints from their parents' failure clusters (Actian VectorAI) and must pass a six-rule fail-closed gate: price band, mutation delta, niche allowlist, spend cap on concurrently deployed capital, parent solvency. Every decision emits a full rule trace.

The honest wrinkle, which is the point: generation 1 improved unit economics roughly 8x while verified accuracy declined. That is reward hacking: early children optimized the declared fitness, not usefulness. The system surfaced it itself, the dashboard reports it as receipt-true successes/attempts, and accuracy-aware selection pressure (evaluation windows, verified sample floors) is the next gate. A market that catches its own reward hacking is the self-evolving story.

Engineering posture

  • 13 modules, each with a hermetic self-test; npm test runs 13/13 green, no keys required.
  • Population snapshots survive restarts (living variants re-register, dead ones re-delist to 410).
  • Append-only JSONL ledgers; ledger truth overrides stale state on the dashboard.
  • Every stubbed integration is labeled local mode, never faked as live.
  • Operator demo triggers exist and are always narrated as operator-triggered.
  • Dashboard footer pins deployed commit + snapshot timestamp.
